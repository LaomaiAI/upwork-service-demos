# Upwork Service Demos

Interactive service demos for my Upwork profile. Each folder is a self-contained
static demo (open its `index.html` in a browser) showing the workflow and
deliverables a client gets — not a live tool.

Upwork profile: https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/~0197d3d04d2369f195

## Demos

| Demo | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| [ai-product-selection](ai-product-selection/) | AI product selection, full workflow from search to factory rank: candidate screening, scoring, sample checks, and factory ranking. |
| [ai-receptionist-interactive](ai-receptionist-interactive/) | Interactive AI receptionist demo for clinics, law firms, and online stores: five playable call scenarios, CRM records, and follow-up samples. |
| [seo-review](seo-review/) | Standalone store SEO + GEO audit in seven steps, including an AI-visibility (GEO) stage with llms.txt and structured data. |
| [roi-attribution](roi-attribution/) | Marketing ROI attribution for a $10,000 monthly budget: CAC, ROAS, ROI, LTV:CAC, break-even point, and payback period. |
| [automation-dev-workflow](automation-dev-workflow/) | Automation and website workflow from client brief to launch in 14 days, with deliverables at each stage. |
| [design-ppt-workflow](design-ppt-workflow/) | PPT design workflow from draft to finished slides, with three style directions and before/after data visualisation. |

## Notes

- All names, numbers, and client stories in these demos are fictional sample

- No demo promises rankings, review counts, or revenue results.
