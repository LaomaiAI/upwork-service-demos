4Stage 2 — Score the products

The eight highest-scoring candidates are scored in full. Every candidate is scored 0–100 on four criteria. The weights reflect what actually kills a new listing: weak demand, a crowded page, thin margin after freight, or a supplier that cannot deliver.

Demand
30%

Search volume trend, seasonality, review velocity on the target marketplace.

Competition
25%

How crowded the first page is. A higher score means a more winnable page.

Margin
25%

Unit cost, freight and fees against target retail. Landed cost is what counts.

Supply risk
20%

Supplier verification, lead time, MOQ flexibility and defect history.

5Stage 3 — Score the physical sample

A spreadsheet score is not enough. For each shortlisted product the client sends a photo and the factory spec sheet, and the physical sample is scored on five checks — material, build quality, packaging, compliance marks, and cost structure. A sample that fails here stops the product, however good the paper score is.

6Stage 4 — Match and compare factories

The sample decides which factories are even worth talking to. Matching runs on four rules, then every matched factory is scored on seven checks: sales volume, reviews and ratings, certifications, years in business, export experience, response rate, and on-time delivery.

Rule 1
Category expertise
The factory must already make this product type, not something next to it.
Rule 2
MOQ fit
Minimum order must fit the client’s first-order budget, not the factory’s ideal.
Rule 3
Export history
Proven shipments to the US or EU, with the paperwork to prove it.
Rule 4
Certifications
The right certificates for the material — checked, not just claimed.

11Recommendation

Launch with the yoga mat as the hero SKU, add the resistance bands as a bundle, and test the dumbbell in a small batch only after the first two are live. That order spends the $8,000 budget where the risk is lowest and the margin is highest, and it keeps one high-ticket test in reserve instead of betting the launch on it.

  1. Weeks 1–2: confirm the two primary factories — Yongkang FitGear for the mat, Yiwu PowerBand for the bands — order samples, run pre-shipment inspection on the bands.
  2. Weeks 3–4: place the first order — 500 mats, 800 band sets. Build the bundle listing and the workout guide.
  3. Weeks 5–8: launch, track return rate and ad cost per order. Order 100 dumbbell sets from Shanghai IronWorks only if the first two SKUs hold their margin targets.
