STAGE 1

Client states requirements

Day 1. The client describes the business in their own words. We turn it into a written brief — no building starts until the brief is agreed.

Sample brief — Maple & Main Plumbing, Austin TX (fictional). "We get most jobs by phone and word of mouth. The website is five years old and does not take bookings. We lose calls after hours and never follow up on quotes we send. We want a site that books jobs, and a system that answers after hours, logs every lead and chases quotes automatically."

STAGE 3

Module-by-module development

Days 3–11. Each module is built against the real requirements from Stage 1 — not a template dropped in and left. The client sees a staging link and can click through progress as it is built.

MODULE A

Website — 5 pages

Mobile-first pages with clear calls to action ("Book a visit", "Call now"), service pages written for local search, click-to-call on every screen, and loading under 2 seconds on a sample 4G test.

MODULE B

Booking & lead capture

Booking form asks for service, preferred slot and address, and confirms instantly by SMS. No lead is ever only in an inbox — every submission is stored and logged.

MODULE C

CRM entry — n8n automation

An n8n workflow creates a HubSpot contact and deal for every lead, tags the source (website, Google Business Profile, phone), and assigns the next action with a due time.

MODULE D

After-hours auto-reply — Twilio

Missed calls after hours trigger an instant SMS reply and a next-morning task for the office. Sample wording agreed with the client in advance, never sent without approval.

MODULE E

Quote follow-up sequence

Quotes move through a day 1 / day 3 / day 7 SMS and email sequence and stop the moment the client replies or books. Every step is logged in the CRM so nothing is chased twice.

MODULE F

Review requests & reporting

Completed jobs trigger a Google review request the next morning, and a weekly report shows leads, bookings, reply times and reviews gained — so the owner sees what the system earned.

Deliverable 3 — Working staging site + automations: a clickable staging link and the live n8n workflows, reviewed with the client before testing.

STAGE 5

Website go-live

Day 14. Launch is a checklist, not a hope. Each item is ticked off in order, and the old site stays up until the new one is verified.

  • Domain and DNS pointed to the new site; old site kept as backup for 7 days
  • SSL certificate active; all pages load over HTTPS
  • Google Business Profile link updated to the new booking page
  • Analytics and Search Console connected; first week of traffic baselined
  • Automations switched from sample data to live mode, one workflow at a time
  • First live booking tested end to end on the real site
  • Owner walkthrough call (30 minutes, recorded): how to check leads, reply, and read the weekly report
Deliverable 5 — Live site + launch record: the site live at the client's domain, with the launch checklist completed and filed.
STAGE 6

Handover — what the client receives

Day 14 and after. The client owns everything. No lock-in, no mystery settings, and 30 days of support to fix anything that comes up.

  • Live website at your domain, with admin access in your name
  • n8n workflows documented step by step, with your accounts (HubSpot, Twilio) connected under your login
  • Booking, CRM, follow-up and review automations running — and a one-page guide for each
  • Weekly report delivered every Monday: leads, bookings, reply time, reviews gained
  • 30 days of fixes included; anything after that is quoted in advance, never billed silently
Deliverable 6 — Handover pack: site, workflows, guides and report schedule — everything above, in your hands.
